# BBAS3 1Q26 Summary

Management acknowledged a materially worsened credit environment and revised guidance downward, emphasizing prudence and risk anticipation. Key changes centered on Cost of credit 2026 and Net Interest Income (NII) 2026. Analyst focus centered on Provision guidance credibility and risk stabilization and Capital adequacy and tax credit sustainability.

## Key Financial and Guidance Highlights
- Cost of credit 2026: raised; investment read-through negative. Management revised cost of credit guidance upward, incorporating higher expected losses from agribusiness punctuality deterioration and individuals contamination.
- Net Interest Income (NII) 2026: raised; investment read-through positive. Management revised NII guidance upward by approximately 3 percentage points.
- Adjusted net income 2026: lowered; investment read-through negative. Management implied downward revision of adjusted net income guidance through discussion of lower profitability expectations and higher provisions.
- Capital / CET1 2026: reaffirmed; investment read-through neutral. Management reaffirmed target CET1 of approximately 11% by end of 2026, with a range of 11-11.5%.

## Temporal Context
Recurring historical themes: Cost of credit 2026 and Net Interest Income (NII) 2026. New or escalating themes: Dividend sustainability and capital management flexibility and Adjusted net income 1Q26 vs Bloomberg/LSEG consensus.

## Key Themes From Analyst Q&A
- Antônio Ruette (Bank of America): Provision guidance credibility and risk stabilization; quality adequate. Response: Management explained that 1Q provisions already incorporated the anticipated risk from April punctuality deterioration and contamination effects.
- Daniel Vaz (Safra): Capital adequacy and tax credit sustainability; quality strong. Response: Management stated that 1314 will remain in effect for five years.
- Gustavo Schroden (Citi): Dividend sustainability and capital management flexibility; quality strong. Response: Management stated that dividends are set at a minimum of 30% based on three-year projections.

The call showed notable divergence from pre-call expectations.
- Adjusted net income 1Q26 vs Bloomberg/LSEG consensus: Management reported adjusted net income of R$3.4bn for 1Q26.
- Cost of credit guidance revision magnitude and direction: Management revised cost of credit guidance upward.
- Contamination beyond agribusiness into individuals credit card segment: Management explicitly highlighted that individuals credit card deterioration is driven by both contamination from rural producer customers and broader household.
